  demonstrated significant technical and usability progress:

  • Composite UX/UI Score increased from 81 / 100 to 87 / 100 (+6 Net Points).

    were resolved via dedicated client scripts (QA-03 and QA-02 in helix.js).

    confirm popups, and UX-05 empty state CTAs).
