Open questions for the lawyer (10)

  • LQ-NIGHT (PF-Q1): Does the Art. 74 night premium (21:00–03:00, +50%) apply only to overtime hours or to all hours worked in the window? How are "shift workers" (excluded) defined? H3LIX assumes approved overtime minutes are the last minutes before clock-out. Until answered: Overtime minutes inside 21:00–03:00 at 1.50; shift-worker exclusion is not applied automatically.
  • LQ-RD (PF-Q2): Art. 75: for a monthly-paid worker whose rest day is already paid, is rest-day work paid at 2.5 × or 1.5 × the basic hourly rate on top of the salary? When may the employer instead grant only normal pay plus a substitute rest day? Until answered: 2.50 × basic hourly rate plus a substitute-rest-day warning.
  • LQ-PH (PF-Q3): Art. 78 (work on a public holiday) refers to Art. 74. Is the premium +25% (+50% at night) of the basic wage, or the Art. 75 rule (+150% or a substitute day)? Until answered: 1.25 (literal reading); configurable 1.25–5.
  • LQ-DED (PF-Q4): Art. 70 caps: is "wage" the gross monthly wage incl. overtime and variable pay or the contractual wage? Do statutory GRSIA contributions and unpaid-leave non-payment count towards the 50%? Are salary advances "loans" for the 10% cap? May disciplinary deductions above 5 days (Art. 60) be carried forward? Until answered: Wage = gross earnings − unpaid leave; GRSIA excluded; advances included; disciplinary excess dropped; loan/advance instalments carried forward whole.
  • LQ-SI (PF-Q5): Law No. 1/2022: exact commencement date; contribution salary for partial months and unpaid leave; meaning of "social allowance"; effect of the Art. 3 one-year permanent-job condition; any State subsidy of the employer share (Art. 11); interaction with the Art. 54 gratuity. Until answered: Contributions from rule set QA v2 (2026-09-08), pro-rated by calendar days, unpaid leave does not reduce the basis.
  • LQ-GCC (PF-Q5): GCC nationals (Law No. 4/2007, amended by Law No. 5/2021 — amendment not read): which home-state rates apply and how is the employer share limited to the Qatari rate? Until answered: Not computed; the run shows a warning for each GCC national.
  • LQ-WPS (PF-Q6): MD No. 50/2026: is the wage for month M due on the 1st of month M+1, and is the 7-day WPS window counted from that day (transfer by the 8th)? Until answered: Due on the 1st of the next month; WPS transfer by due date + 7 days.
  • LQ-MW (PF-Q7): Is the QAR 1,000 minimum wage (MD No. 25/2020) the "basic wage" under Art. 1 and Art. 54? Any revision since 2020? Until answered: Salary entry refuses a basic wage below QAR 1,000 (QA-MW-01).
  • LQ-GR (PF-Q8): Gratuity day rate: monthly basic ÷ 30 (Art. 8), ÷ 26 or × 12 ÷ 365? Are the 3 "weeks" 21 calendar days or 15/18 working days? Until answered: basic ÷ 30 × 21 days per year of service, pro-rated for fractions of a year.
  • LQ-LEAVE: Leave encashment (Art. 81) and annual-leave pay: basic wage only, or basic plus fixed allowances? Until answered: Basic only; components flagged "in leave-pay basis" are added (schema 2).
