IDRequirementLegal sourceH3LIXStatus
QA-MW-01Basic wage at least QAR 1,000 per month. Law No. 17/2020; MD No. 25/2020 Art. 2 Rule min_wage_basic_monthly; refused at offer creation and salary-structure entry; live check of current structures.Implemented & tested
QA-MW-02If housing / food are not provided in kind, allowances of at least QAR 500 / QAR 300 per month. MD No. 25/2020 Art. 2 Values configured; salary structures do not record whether housing/food are provided in kind, so the allowance minimum is not validated.Gap
QA-WP-01Pay at least monthly into a Qatar bank account via WPS within 7 days of the due date; since 8 Sep 2026 the monthly wage is due on the 1st. Labour Law Art. 66; MD No. 4/2015 Art. 2 as replaced by MD No. 50/2026 Payroll enabled per company after the setup wizard (brief 2026-10-13); rule v2 wage_due_day_monthly/wage_transfer_days; each run shows the WPS due date, transfer deadline and overdue status (RunService::wpsStatus); SIF export; not independently validated (LQ-WPS).Implemented & tested
QA-WP-02Salary Information File format accepted by the bank. Bank / QCB SIF specification (not a legal text — secondary) Payroll SIF adapter; field layout not verified against a bank specification.Partial
QA-DC-01Disciplinary deductions at most 5 days' wage per offence and per month; penalties register. Labour Law Arts. 58–60 Payroll caps disciplinary deductions at 5 days' wage per month (DeductionCaps, input category 'disciplinary'); no disciplinary module / penalties register yet.Partial
QA-DC-02Total deductions at most 50% of the wage; employer-loan repayments interest-free and at most 10% of the wage. Labour Law Art. 70 Payroll engine enforces the caps: loan/advance instalments above 10% of the wage are carried forward; total deductions above 50% raise a blocking warning that stops submission (LQ-DED open).Implemented & tested
QA-WR-01Wage register: wages, supplements, overtime, deductions and net wage per worker. Labour Law Art. 48(2) Payroll runs and entries hold these values; payroll enabled per company by the setup wizard (brief 2026-10-13).Implemented & tested
QA-WH-01Normal hours at most 8 a day / 48 a week; in Ramadan 6 a day / 36 a week. Labour Law Art. 73 Shift templates longer than 8 net hours are flagged as overtime; Ramadan dates and the weekly total are not yet checked.Partial
QA-WH-02Breaks of 1–3 hours in total; no more than 5 consecutive hours of work. Labour Law Art. 73 Break minutes recorded on shift templates; the 5-consecutive-hours rule is not checked.Gap
QA-OT-01Overtime at least +25%; work between 21:00 and 03:00 at least +50% (not for shift workers). Labour Law Art. 74 Payroll v2: overtime +25%; overtime minutes 21:00–03:00 on a night line at 1.50 (PF-Q1). Shift workers not excluded automatically; LQ-NIGHT open.Partial
QA-OT-02Actual work at most 10 hours a day including overtime (except to prevent serious loss or accident). Labour Law Art. 74 Attendance days over 10 h get the exception overtime_over_limit for the approver; live check counts them.Implemented & tested
QA-RD-01Weekly paid rest of at least 24 consecutive hours (normally Friday); work on the rest day: substitute rest day and basic wage + at least 150%. Labour Law Art. 75 Rest days in schedules and leave; payroll v2 pays rest-day work at 2.50 × basic hourly rate with a warning that a substitute rest day is required (PF-Q2, LQ-RD open); substitute day not tracked.Partial
QA-HS-01No outdoor work under the sun 10:00–15:30 from 1 June to 15 September. MD No. 17/2021 Art. 2 Outdoor flag on shift templates; such assignments are refused, held back at publication and counted in live checks.Implemented & tested
QA-HS-02Monitor workplace climate and stop work above WBGT 32.1 °C. MD No. 17/2021 Art. 4(8)–(9) Site measurement is outside HR software; value recorded for reference.Not applicable to HR software
QA-HS-03Annual medical examinations (heat stress) and periodic medical results kept in the personnel file. MD No. 17/2021 Art. 4; Labour Law Art. 105 Document type "Medical fitness certificate" with reminders; no schedule of examinations.Partial
QA-HS-04Work-injury register, immediate notification, six-monthly injury statistics. Labour Law Arts. 48(4), 108, 115; MD No. 18/2005 Not implemented.Gap
QA-LV-01Annual leave after one year: at least 3 weeks (4 weeks after 5 years), pro-rata for fractions. Labour Law Art. 79 Annual leave types (code AL) below 3 weeks are refused (weeks converted by the type's weekend rule — interpretation pending); live check. The 4-week tier is not yet applied automatically.Partial
QA-LV-02Leave split into at most two parts with consent; at most half carried to the next year at the worker's request. Labour Law Art. 80 Carry-forward maximum per leave type exists; the two-part and half limits are not enforced.Gap
QA-LV-03Leave pay before the leave; cash for untaken leave on termination; no waiver. Labour Law Arts. 68, 81 Leave encashment in payroll and final settlement (payroll not enabled).Partial
QA-PH-0110 paid public holidays: Eid al-Fitr 3, Eid al-Adha 3, National Day 1, 3 set by the employer. Labour Law Art. 78 (as amended by Law No. 9/2026) Public holidays per company; live check of companies with fewer than 10 configured this year.Implemented & tested
QA-SL-01Sick leave after 3 months: 2 weeks full pay, 4 weeks half pay, then unpaid; certificate from an employer-approved doctor. Labour Law Art. 82 Values configured; leave types do not model pay tiers yet.Gap
QA-ML-01Maternity leave 50 days full pay after one year of service (at least 35 days after delivery). Labour Law Art. 96 Values configured; no maternity leave type rules yet.Gap
QA-ML-02Nursing break of 1 hour a day for one year after maternity leave. Labour Law Art. 97 Not implemented.Gap
QA-HJ-01Unpaid Hajj leave up to 20 days, once during service, for Muslim workers. Labour Law Art. 83 Values configured; not enforced on a leave type.Gap
QA-OL-01No statutory bereavement, marriage or paternity leave in the private-sector Labour Law. Labour Law Chapters 7 and 9 Companies may still add such leave types.Not applicable to HR software
QA-PR-01Probation at most 6 months, once per employer; 1 month's written notice by the employer. Labour Law Art. 39 (DL No. 18/2020) Contract registration refuses longer probation; onboarding caps probation and extensions; live check of employees on probation beyond 6 months.Implemented & tested
QA-NT-01Notice 1 month (up to 2 years of service) or 2 months (after 2 years), both parties, in writing. Labour Law Art. 49 (DL No. 18/2020) Resignations and terminations with shorter notice are flagged; transfers / job changes compute the Art. 39/49/51 notice as data, keep the Ministry date as authoritative (brief 2026-10-13 §3).Implemented & tested
QA-NT-02Summary dismissal only on the listed grounds; worker may leave without notice in listed cases. Labour Law Arts. 51, 61 (Law No. 9/2026 adds ground 11) Separation types exist; grounds are not recorded or checked.Gap
QA-JC-01Change of employer without consent after notice; probation transfer compensation ≤ 2 months' basic; residence permit valid or ≤ 90 days expired; new contract authenticated before the start date. Labour Law Arts. 39, 49, 52, 54, 67; Law No. 21/2015 Arts. 21, 23 (DL 19/2020); MoI Exec. Reg. Arts. 65, 83 Transfers module (brief 2026-10-13 §3): internal transfer with two-company HR approval, outgoing and incoming job changes, notice table as data, government checklist gating the start (override recorded), continuity vs settlement, pro-rata payroll split. Research docs/compliance/qatar-job-change-research.md; 16 lawyer questions.Partial
QA-GR-01End-of-service gratuity after one year: at least 3 weeks' last basic wage per year, pro-rata. Labour Law Art. 54 Final settlement in payroll (rule v2, divisor configurable — LQ-GR); service from the EOS start date (opening balance / transfer continuity) or hire date; transfers choose continuity or settlement with a recorded legal note.Implemented & tested
QA-FS-01All dues paid by the end of the working day after the contract ends (7 days if the worker left without notice). Labour Law Art. 67 Live check of completed separations without an approved settlement after the deadline; transfers with settlement and outgoing job changes prepare the final settlement on completion with the Art. 67 deadline note.Implemented & tested
QA-WA-01Minimum working age 16. Labour Law Art. 86 Employees younger than 16 on the hire date are refused.Implemented & tested
QA-WA-02Juveniles (16–17): permit, medical checks, 6 h/day, no night work, prohibited work. Labour Law Arts. 87–92 Not implemented.Gap
QA-WO-01Equal pay for women; prohibited work and permitted hours set by ministerial decision. Labour Law Arts. 93–95 The implementing decision on women's hours could not be verified; not implemented.Gap
QA-CT-01Written contract in Arabic, three copies, attested by the Department, with the mandatory particulars. Labour Law Arts. 9, 38 Contracts registered per employee with the signed copy as a document; live check of active employees without a contract. Language and attestation are not tracked.Partial
QA-CT-02Fixed-term contracts at most 5 years. Labour Law Art. 40 Longer fixed-term contracts are refused at registration.Implemented & tested
QA-PF-01Personnel file per worker kept at least one year after the end of service. Labour Law Art. 47 Documents and records are kept (soft delete only, nothing pruned automatically); retention setting cannot be below 1 year.Implemented & tested
QA-RG-01Workers register (name, nationality, job, wage, start date…) and six-monthly workforce statements. Labour Law Arts. 19, 48(1) Report "Workforce statement (Qatar)" (without wages; payroll not enabled).Partial
QA-QZ-01Localisation: six-monthly workforce data, vacancies within 1 month, hires within 60 days. Law No. 12/2024 Workforce statement report provides the data; submission and vacancy notifications are outside H3LIX.Partial
QA-RP-01Employer handles residence permits and renews them within 90 days of expiry. Law No. 21/2015 Arts. 8, 10 Typed QID / residence / work-permit documents with expiry, reminders at 90/60/30/0 days, dashboard and directory flags, live checks.Implemented & tested
QA-RP-02Passports are returned to the worker (safekeeping only on written request). Law No. 21/2015 Art. 8 H3LIX stores document copies only; physical custody is not tracked.Partial
QA-SI-01Qatari employees: 7% employee + 14% employer contributions on basic + social + housing allowance (cap QAR 100,000; housing up to QAR 6,000), paid by the 5th. Law No. 1/2022 Arts. 11–12 Payroll v2 calculates GRSIA for Qatari employees: 7% employee deduction and 14% employer line on components flagged for the GRSIA basis (housing ≤ 6,000, cap 100,000); wizard step 4 records the employer registration. GCC nationals flagged, not calculated (LQ-GCC); settlements do not deduct GRSIA yet.Partial
QA-DP-01Lawful, transparent processing with notice to the individual. Law No. 13/2016 Arts. 3–4, 9 (PDPPL) Privacy notice page (template, legal review pending) linked from every page.Partial
QA-DP-02Right of access to one's personal data. Law No. 13/2016 Arts. 5–6 Self-service download and HR export (privacy.export) of everything H3LIX holds, audited.Implemented & tested
QA-DP-03Appropriate security; protect sensitive data. Law No. 13/2016 Arts. 8, 11–13 Company/team scope, encrypted identity numbers with logged reveals, private documents with logged downloads, MFA, audit log.Implemented & tested
QA-DP-04Keep personal data no longer than necessary. Law No. 13/2016 Art. 10 Retention period per company for leavers; leavers past it are reported (never deleted automatically).Implemented & tested
QA-DP-05Notify breaches affecting individuals. Law No. 13/2016 Art. 14 (breach timing: secondary sources only) Audit trail supports investigation; no breach-notification workflow.Gap
QA-DP-06Do not restrict lawful cross-border flows; assess transfers. Law No. 13/2016 Art. 15 Depends on the hosting location chosen per customer deployment (operator decision).Gap
QA-DP-07Special-nature data (health, religious beliefs, marital relationship, children, criminal offences…) may be processed only with a permit from the competent department. Law No. 13/2016 Art. 16 H3LIX can hold such data (medical certificates, health cards, Hajj leave). Whether the employer needs a permit is a legal question; no permit tracking.Gap
